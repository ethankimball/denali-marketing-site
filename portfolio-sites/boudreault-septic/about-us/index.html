Boudreault Septic history

1952

Patrick starts the business

Patrick Boudreault began as an active plumber, septic installer, and excavator. He built the company around skilled work and service to his neighbors.

1969

John Sr. returns home

After returning from Vietnam, John Sr. took over the business. He continued the same services and grew the company to ten employees with multiple service trucks.

Today

The third generation carries it forward

John Boudreault Jr. has focused the company on septic installation and maintenance while keeping the family standard intact. The next generation already keeps an eye on the backhoe work.

The name on the truck matters.

Family ownership keeps the work personal. Calls get answered, local history stays on file, and every finished job carries the Boudreault name.

Today the company serves homeowners, commercial properties, and municipalities across more than 30 towns in New Hampshire and Vermont.
