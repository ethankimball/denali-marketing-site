Common questions

Why do I need to pump my tank?

Sludge builds up in the tank. If it reaches the leach field, it can cause the system to fail.

How do I find my tank or access cover?

Call us. We may have a map on file, and we can also locate the tank electronically.

How often should I pump my tank?

We recommend every 1-3 years, depending on system size and household use.
